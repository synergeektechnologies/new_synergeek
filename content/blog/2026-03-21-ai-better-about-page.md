Many small business owners know they *should* have a good About page, but when they sit down to write it, they freeze. It feels awkward to write about yourself. You’re busy. And most examples you see online sound either boring, over-the-top, or full of buzzwords.

The good news: modern AI writing tools can act like a friendly assistant sitting next to you, helping you turn your thoughts into clear, customer-friendly words. You still stay in control of the message, but the AI helps you get unstuck, clean up the phrasing, and sound more confident.

This guide will show you, step by step, how to use AI to create a better About page — even if you don’t like writing and you’re not "techy" at all.

## What you’ll learn

By the end of this article, you’ll know how to:

- Turn your story and experience into clear, simple text using AI
- Avoid generic, "copy-paste" About pages that sound like everyone else
- Give AI the *right kind* of instructions so the result still sounds like you
- Quickly improve an existing About page without starting from scratch

You don’t need any coding or marketing background. If you can type a message into a chat box or form, you can do this.

## Why your About page matters more than you think

Many business owners treat the About page as an afterthought. But for potential customers, it’s often one of the most important pages on your site.

People click on About pages when they’re asking questions like:

- "Can I trust this person or company?"
- "Do they really understand my situation?"
- "Are they local / small / independent, or just another faceless brand?"

A good About page doesn’t just list dates and awards. It builds a connection. It shows that there are real humans behind the logo. It makes visitors feel, "These are my kind of people."

AI can help you express that in a clear, friendly way — especially if you’re not sure how to talk about yourself without feeling silly.

## Step 1: Collect simple notes before you ask AI for help

AI works best when you give it clear information to work with. Before you open any AI tool, take 5–10 minutes to jot down bullet points about your business.

You can write this in a document, a notebook, or directly in the AI chat later. Keep it simple:

- **Who you are** – e.g., "I’m a freelance graphic designer in Coimbatore," or "We’re a two-person bakery that started in 2019."
- **Who you serve** – e.g., "local service businesses," "parents in my neighborhood," or "small ecommerce shops."
- **What problem you solve** – e.g., "help businesses look more professional online," "provide fresh, affordable cakes for celebrations," etc.
- **Your story** – Why did you start? What do customers usually appreciate about you?
- **Your tone** – Do you want to sound friendly, professional, playful, calm, or something else?

You don’t need perfect sentences. Even rough notes like these are enough for AI to turn into a clear first draft.

## Step 2: Give AI a clear prompt (instructions)

Once you have your notes, open your preferred AI writing tool. Most tools work in a similar way: you type instructions, and the AI replies with text.

Copy-paste your notes and then give a clear instruction like this:

> "I run a small [type of business] in [city]. Please write a friendly, simple About page for my website in plain English. Use a warm but professional tone. Focus on who I help, what problems I solve, and why customers like working with me. Keep paragraphs short and avoid buzzwords. Here are my notes: [paste your bullet points]."

This kind of prompt tells the AI exactly what you want: **simple language, clear structure, and a focus on the customer.**

If the first version it gives you sounds too formal or robotic, don’t worry. You can always reply with:

> "Make this sound more casual and conversational, as if I’m talking to a friendly customer in person."

Think of AI like a junior writer: it will do the heavy lifting, but you still guide the style.

## Step 3: Make sure the About page is customer-focused

A common mistake with About pages is talking only about *yourself*:

> "We were founded in 2015. We have 10 years of experience. We are passionate about excellence."  
> (You’ve probably seen this kind of thing.)

Instead, you want your About page to connect your story to your customer’s world. After the AI gives you a draft, check for these points:

- **Does it clearly say who you help?** A visitor should quickly see if they’re your ideal customer.
- **Does it mention the problems or goals your customers have?** For example, "busy parents," "small business owners who don’t have time for marketing," etc.
- **Does it show how your background helps them?** Not just "I have 8 years of experience," but "I’ve helped many local businesses move from word-of-mouth to online leads."

If the draft is too "me, me, me," you can ask the AI:

> "Rewrite this so it speaks more directly to the reader. Add lines that show I understand their worries and goals."

This tiny tweak often makes a big difference.

## Step 4: Add human details only you can provide

AI is good at structure and clarity, but it doesn’t know your life unless you tell it. To make your About page feel real and not generic, add a few specific details:

- How you got started (a short story)
- What you enjoy most about serving your customers
- One or two values that guide your work (e.g., honesty, punctuality, simplicity)

You can type to the AI:

> "Add a short paragraph about why I started this business. I began in 2020 after friends kept asking me to design logos, and I realized there was a need for affordable, reliable design help for small local businesses."

Or simply edit the text yourself and insert these details. The goal is for a visitor to read the page and think, "This sounds like a real person, not a template."

## Step 5: Check for clarity and plain language

Because many AI tools were trained on the public internet, they sometimes copy the style of corporate websites: long sentences, buzzwords, and vague claims.

Before you publish your About page, read it out loud. Ask yourself:

- Would I actually say this to a customer face to face?
- Are there any phrases that feel fake or too fancy for my usual style?
- Are there long sentences I can break into two shorter ones?

You can also tell the AI:

> "Simplify the language so a 12-year-old could understand it. Remove buzzwords and keep it friendly and direct."

This doesn’t make you sound childish — it makes you sound clear. And clarity builds trust.

## Step 6: Add a clear next step at the end

Many About pages leave visitors hanging. They learn about you, but then… what next?

Use AI to help you add a simple call-to-action at the bottom. For example:

- "If this sounds like what you need, the easiest next step is to [book a free call / send me a WhatsApp message / fill out this form]."
- "Ready to get started? Visit our Services page to see how we can work together."

You can ask the AI:

> "Add a short, friendly call-to-action at the end that invites visitors to contact me by [your preferred channel]."

This way, your About page doesn’t just build trust — it gently guides people towards working with you.

## Step 7: Keep it updated with small AI-assisted tweaks

Your business will change over time. You might add new services, focus on a different type of client, or shift from part-time to full-time.

Instead of rewriting your About page from scratch every year, use AI for small updates:

1. Paste your current About page into the AI.
2. Share what has changed (e.g., "Now I also offer social media templates," or "I’ve started working with more clients outside my city.").
3. Ask it to update the text while keeping your tone and main message.

For example:

> "Here is my current About page. Please update it to mention that I now work with clients across India via online meetings. Keep the same friendly tone and structure."

This keeps your website fresh without taking hours of your time.

## Final thoughts

You don’t need to be a professional writer to have a strong About page. With a few simple notes and clear instructions, AI can help you:

- Turn your story into clear, confident writing
- Show visitors you understand their needs
- Build trust without sounding arrogant or fake

The key is to stay in control: **you provide the truth, AI helps with the words.**

If your current About page is empty, outdated, or just a few lines, this is a great place to start improving your online presence. Set aside 30–45 minutes, open an AI writing tool, and follow the steps in this guide.

Your future customers are already checking your About page. With a little AI help, you can make sure it gives them a clear, friendly picture of who you are and why you’re the right choice for them.
